How I work

I lead data engineering on IVADO Labs client projects, mostly in transport, aerospace, travel and enterprise software. A typical engagement starts with messy source data and ends with something a team runs every day.

That means pipelines with checks for freshness, schema drift and flatlined sensors; datasets versioned so models can rely on them while the sources keep changing; and models tracked in MLflow and served behind an API.

I write typed, linted, CI-gated Python, provision with Terraform, and use Claude coding agents for implementation, refactoring and tests, inside the same checks as everything else. I also mentor engineers and set review and documentation practice on my teams.

I started in Sfax, Tunisia, worked in France, and have lived in Montréal since 2016. I work in French, English and Arabic.
